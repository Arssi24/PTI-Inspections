Privacy Policy

YLM Record PTI · last updated November 23, 2026

YLM Record PTI is the inspection app of YLM Logistics Inc. ("we", "us"). Drivers use it to record pre-trip inspections (PTI) and trailer drops and hooks. Fleet managers use it to review them. This page explains what the app collects, why, and how long it is kept.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, login (email), phone number, and the fleet you belong to. Your password is stored in scrambled (hashed) form. Nobody, including us, can read it.
  • Each inspection you submit: the truck or trailer number, the type (PTI, Drop or Hook), the date and time, the length of the recording, the video with sound you record, any photos you mark during the video, and the GPS location of your phone at the moment you submit it (only if you allow location access).
  • On your phone: the app keeps your login session and any recording that has not finished uploading in the phone's own storage so the app works with a weak signal. This is not a tracking cookie.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising pixels, session replay or tracking cookies.
  • We do not collect ID photos, selfies or any biometric information.

Why we collect it

To document vehicle walkaround inspections, keep the fleet safe and compliant, and let managers see which units were checked, by whom and when.

Who can see it

  • You can see your own inspections.
  • Fleet managers of your fleet can see every inspection, video, photo, driver name, phone number and location for their fleet.
  • Service providers that run the app for us: Supabase (database, login and file storage) and GitHub Pages (website hosting). They process data only to provide those services.
  • We may share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Inspection records, videos and photos are deleted automatically about 14 days after they are recorded.
  • Your account stays until your fleet manager removes it. When a manager removes a driver, that person's login is deleted.

Recording and sound

Inspection videos include sound. Please record only the vehicle inspection and do not record private conversations of other people.

Your choices

  • Ask your fleet manager to correct your details or remove your account.
  • You can turn off location access for the app in your phone settings. The inspection can still be submitted without it.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Access is limited by login and by fleet: a driver can only see their own inspections, and a manager can only see their own fleet. Uploads are limited to video and photo files.

Who may use the app

The app is for people working with YLM Logistics Inc. and its partner fleets. It is not meant for children.

Changes and questions

We may update this page and will change the date above when we do. For questions or requests, ask your fleet manager or contact YLM Logistics Inc.

This policy is a plain-language summary of how the app works. It should be reviewed by a lawyer before the app is offered to other companies.
